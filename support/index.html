Support

Need help with one of our apps, found a bug, or have an idea? Email us and a real person will get back to you.

hello@kdfarmandgarden.com

It helps if you include:

  • Which app you're using
  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • What happened, and a screenshot if you can

We usually reply within a few business days.

Deleting your data

Our apps keep your work on your device. Deleting the app removes it. If you've sent us an email or beta feedback and want it deleted, just ask at the address above.
