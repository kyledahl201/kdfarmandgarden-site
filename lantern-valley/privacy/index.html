Lantern Valley Coloring Book: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026
Last updated: November 17, 2026

Lantern Valley Coloring Book is a coloring app made for children. We built it so that it collects nothing at all.

The short version

  • We don't collect, store or share any personal information.
  • There are no accounts, sign-ins, ads, tracking or analytics.
  • The app doesn't connect to our servers or to any third-party service.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

What stays on your device

Your child's colored pages and progress are saved only on your iPad. We never receive them. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple may include them in your device backup under Apple's own privacy policy, and we can't see that backup.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are bought, billed, renewed and canceled through your Apple Account. Apple doesn't give us your name, email or payment details. We see only anonymous sales totals from Apple. To manage or cancel a subscription, go to Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, use Restore Purchases in the app's grown-ups area.

Apple's optional diagnostics

If you've chosen to share app analytics with developers in your iPad's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash and usage statistics. These don't identify you or your child. You can turn this off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

The app is designed for children ages 5 and under and is listed in the App Store's Kids category. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, there's nothing to review, change or delete. Purchases, the Terms and Privacy links, and anything else meant for adults sit behind a grown-ups check.

Voices and art

The voices and artwork in the app are built into it. The app doesn't use the microphone or camera, and it doesn't record your child.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, we'll update this page and change the effective date before that version is released. We won't begin collecting children's personal information without first asking a parent for consent, as the law requires.
