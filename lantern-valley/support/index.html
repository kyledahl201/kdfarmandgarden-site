Lantern Valley Coloring Book: Help for grown-ups

Lantern Valley Coloring Book is a coloring app for iPad.

Contact

Need help? Email hello@kdfarmandgarden.com. We usually reply within a few business days.

Lantern Valley Pass

  • Manage or cancel: Subscriptions are billed by Apple. To manage or cancel, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.
  • Refunds: Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Restore: On a new iPad, open the app's grown-ups area and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account.
  • Family Sharing: With Family Sharing on, family members can use the subscription on their own devices.
  • The farm pages stay free, and colored pages stay saved on your iPad, with or without the Pass.

Common questions

  • No sound? Check the iPad's volume, then the sound switches in the app's grown-ups area.
  • My child keeps leaving the app. Guided Access (Settings, Accessibility, Guided Access) keeps the iPad on one app.
  • Start over? Reset progress is in the grown-ups area. It clears the colored pages on that iPad and can't be undone.
