GutterGuide Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

GutterGuide is made by KD Farm and Garden LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("we", "us"). GutterGuide is a measuring tool for gutter and roofing work ("you" are the person using it).

What we collect

What Why Where it's kept
Job addresses you enter To find the home on the map On your device
Measurements, diagrams and notes you create To save your jobs On your device

GutterGuide doesn't have accounts, so your jobs stay on your device and we don't receive them. To measure a roof, the app sends only the home's map coordinates (latitude and longitude) to the services below, never your name, your notes or the address text. We don't collect your location in the background, and we don't use tracking, analytics or advertising tools. If we add accounts or syncing later, we'll update this policy before that feature ships.

Homeowner information

The addresses you enter belong to homes you're working on. We don't contact homeowners, we don't market to them, and we don't sell or rent addresses or job data to anyone. You're responsible for having a legitimate reason to enter an address.

Services the app uses

  • Apple Maps (MapKit) receives the address text you type so it can find the location. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • Our roof-data service, hosted on Google Cloud, receives the home's map coordinates to look up public elevation data. It doesn't store the coordinates or tie them to you. Like any web service, Google Cloud keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and time) for up to 30 days for security and troubleshooting.
  • Public map data sources receive the home's map coordinates to fetch public elevation data and building outlines: the U.S. Geological Survey's National Map, OpenStreetMap's Overpass service, and public datasets hosted on Amazon Web Services (and, if added, Microsoft's public building footprints). Some of these are contacted by our roof-data service rather than your phone.
  • Apple handles any App Store purchases. We never see your card details.

We may disclose information if the law requires it. If our company is ever sold, the buyer would be bound by this policy.

Beta testing (TestFlight)

While GutterGuide is in beta, KD Farm and Garden LLC distributes it through Apple's TestFlight. If you join the beta:

  • Apple shares with us your name and email (from your TestFlight invitation), your device model and iOS version, crash reports, and any feedback, comments or screenshots you choose to send through TestFlight. Screenshots may show whatever is on your screen, including addresses, so don't send ones you don't want us to see.
  • We use it only to fix bugs, improve GutterGuide and contact you about the beta.
  • Test only homes you have permission to measure, such as your own home or a friend's with their OK. Don't enter addresses of customers of any company you work for.
  • When the beta ends, or if you ask to leave it, we delete your tester information and beta feedback within 90 days, unless you become a customer.
  • Apple's handling of TestFlight data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Your jobs stay on your device until you delete them or delete the app. Our roof-data service doesn't keep coordinates; standard request logs roll off within 30 days. Beta tester information is deleted as described above.

Your choices and rights

You can delete any job in the app at any time. To ask about, correct or delete tester information we hold, email us. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under state privacy laws. We'll honor them, and we won't treat you differently for using them.

Children

GutterGuide isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We'll post any changes here, with a new effective date, before they take effect.
